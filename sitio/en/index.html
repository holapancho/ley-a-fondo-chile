Chilean law, cited article by article.

A skill for your AI agent that downloads a law from Ley Chile, the official legal database of Chile's National Congress Library (BCN), indexes it on your computer and answers with every statement cited: article, version date and official link. Before answering, the agent checks its citations against the official text.

› Does an accident on the way to work count as a work accident?

Yes. Law 16.744 calls it a commuting accident (accidente de trayecto):

«Son también accidentes del trabajo los ocurridos en el trayecto directo, de ida o regreso, entre la habitación y el lugar del trabajo»

Ley 16.744, art. 5 (texto desde 2006-04-28; versión de la norma 2021-12-10)

In short: accidents on the direct route between home and work, in either direction, are work accidents. Accidents caused by force majeure unrelated to the job, or caused intentionally by the victim, are excluded (Ley 16.744, art. 5).

Hypothetical: a worker falls off her bike on her usual route from home to the office. It is a commuting accident.

How it works

Everything runs on your computer with a single Node.js script and no dependencies. The skill only downloads what you ask for.

Ask for a law

"Load law 20.000" or "load the labor code". The skill downloads it from Ley Chile with all its regulations and indexes it in ~/.ley-a-fondo-chile/.

Ask your question

The agent searches the index, reads the full articles, follows cross-references and regulations, and translates penalties by citing the Criminal Code.

It verifies

Before answering, the agent checks that every cited article exists, that every link points to the article it names, and that every quote in «» matches the official text exactly.

It answers with citations

A summary table, case-by-case detail, the literal text of penalties and examples labeled as hypothetical. If the law does not answer the question, it says so.

Install

Pick your agent. The skill needs Node.js 24 or later and internet access to leychile.cl.

As a plugin, from this repository's marketplace. Inside Claude Code:

/plugin marketplace add holapancho/ley-a-fondo-chile
/plugin install ley-a-fondo-chile@ley-a-fondo-chile

Or from the terminal:

claude plugin marketplace add holapancho/ley-a-fondo-chile
claude plugin install ley-a-fondo-chile@ley-a-fondo-chile

To update: claude plugin update ley-a-fondo-chile@ley-a-fondo-chile.

With the Agent Skills installer (npx skills), which detects the agents you have installed:

npx skills add holapancho/ley-a-fondo-chile

For a specific agent, or for all your projects (-g):

npx skills add holapancho/ley-a-fondo-chile -a codex -g
npx skills add holapancho/ley-a-fondo-chile -a cursor
npx skills add holapancho/ley-a-fondo-chile -a claude-code -g

The npx skills installer requires Node.js 22.20 or later.

  1. Download ley-a-fondo-chile.zip (latest version).
  2. In claude.ai, open settings, find the Skills section and upload the .zip.
  3. Code execution must be enabled, with internet access to leychile.cl and nuevo.leychile.cl. On organization accounts, an admin enables this.

Copy the skills/ley-a-fondo-chile folder into your agent's skills folder. For example, in Claude Code:

git clone https://github.com/holapancho/ley-a-fondo-chile
cp -r ley-a-fondo-chile/skills/ley-a-fondo-chile ~/.claude/skills/

Check that it works:

node ~/.claude/skills/ley-a-fondo-chile/scripts/ley-a-fondo.mjs estado

What you can ask

Penalties

"What are the penalties for drug possession?" The agent tells apart personal use, small-scale dealing and trafficking, and translates penalty grades by citing the Criminal Code.

Codes

"What does article 18 quáter of the Labor Code say?" or "What are the sources of obligations under the Civil Code?"

Decrees and regulations

Any law, decree, DFL or decree-law published in Ley Chile, together with the regulations that implement it and its annexes.

Known limitations

Current version only

Earlier versions of a law cannot be consulted.

Tables as images

Some tables, like the penalty scale in article 56 of the Criminal Code, are published as images. Their data cannot be verified as text, and the agent warns about it.

Laws outside Ley Chile

Rules not published in Ley Chile, such as those of the CMF (the financial regulator), cannot be loaded. For some circulars, BCN only publishes an excerpt.

Undocumented service

The skill uses the same public endpoints as the Ley Chile website. Since they are undocumented, they could change; a weekly test warns if that happens.

Legal notice

Answers are not an official interpretation by the National Congress Library (BCN) and not legal advice. For a specific case, consult a lawyer.

Legal texts come from Ley Chile – Biblioteca del Congreso Nacional. Every answer cites the source and the date the text was retrieved. The skill only downloads what you ask for and does not replicate Ley Chile.

This project is independent and not affiliated with the National Congress Library.
